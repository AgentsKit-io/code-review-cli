# Code of Conduct

We want AgentsKit Code Review to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful project.

Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, assume good intent, and make room for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through a maintainer's contact details on the [AgentsKit organization page](https://github.com/AgentsKit-io). Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.

This policy applies in repository discussions and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.
